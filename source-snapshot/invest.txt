The brochure month, times a thousand
100 users × ₱500 × 1,000 sites. Starlink 1 TB at ₱8,400, host ₱2,000, upkeep ₱2,000. The guide rounds operating cost to about ₱12 million and profit to about ₱38 million. The exact calm-month arithmetic is ₱12.4 million of cost and ₱37.6 million of profit.
Suggested terms, once that profit is real and stable in year two: 8–10 investors, and 40–50% of profit as a dividend. That is a planning suggestion from the guide, not a yield and not an offer.

Cash if you build evenly over 24 months
Each new site follows the fill-up curve: 25 users in its first month, 100 by month 7, 500 GB until 50 users, then 1 TB. No collections leak. After month 24 the model stops buying sites and watches for another 12 months. Rates are the guide defaults.
